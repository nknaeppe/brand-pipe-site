How it works

Three steps, about ten seconds per clip.

Post the URL

Your workflow sends the video URL, optionally a logo URL and the target format, with your API key in the X-API-Key header.

We process it

Fit to 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 with clean padding, logo bottom-right with alpha, loudness normalised to −14 LUFS, H.264 with fast start.

Download and publish

You get a signed download link, valid for one hour, from EU storage. Hand it to your upload node and you are done.

The whole API

One endpoint. A JSON envelope you can branch on.

curl -X POST https://api.brand-pipe.com/v1/process \
  -H "X-API-Key: bp_your_key" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "video_url": "https://.../raw.mp4",
    "logo_url":  "https://.../logo.png",
    "format":    "vertical",
    "audio_normalize": true
  }'
{
  "success": true,
  "data": {
    "job_id": "6bbee170…",
    "result_url": "https://…/6bbee170….mp4?X-Amz-…",
    "duration_s": 10.0,
    "had_audio": true,
    "format": "vertical"
  },
  "error": null
}

format

vertical 1080×1920 · square 1080×1080 · landscape 1920×1080

logo_url

PNG with transparency. Scaled to 15 % of the width, 20 px from the corner.

subtitles

An SRT or WebVTT you already have, inline or by URL, burned into the picture. Costs one extra credit.

limits

Up to 3 minutes and 200 MB per video, 10 minutes via /v1/jobs. Public http(s) URLs only. Results are kept for 24 hours.

errors

401 bad key · 402 no credits · 409 idempotency conflict · 413 too large · 422 unreadable video · 424 source download failed · 429 rate limited

Retries never pay twice

Send Idempotency-Key: {{ $execution.id }}-{{ $itemIndex }} and a retried request — n8n's Retry On Fail, a lost response, a timeout — gets the first result back instead of a second charge and a second render. Keys are remembered for 24 hours.

Long clips: submit, then get called back

POST /v1/jobs takes the same body plus an optional callback_url, answers at once with a status_url, and posts the finished job to your URL — an n8n Wait node resumes on it. Polling the status URL is free and always works too.

Free n8n templates

Import, paste your key, run.

The workflow posts a video, waits for the result and downloads the finished file as binary data for your upload node. Tested on n8n 2.37.10.

Import

Download n8n-brand-pipe.json and import it via Workflow → Import from file.

Add the credential

Create a Header Auth credential named exactly Brand-Pipe API Key: header name X-API-Key, value your key. Both templates look the credential up by that name, so it only has to be set up once.

Wire it in

Replace the Set video node with your generator's output and connect the binary output to YouTube, TikTok or your storage.

Want the whole pipeline? n8n-brand-pipe-youtube.json adds a schedule trigger, a placeholder node for your video generator and a YouTube upload step that publishes the result as a Short. The upload node ships disabled so you can run it before connecting a channel.

FAQ

The practical questions.

Where is my data processed and stored?

Processing runs on Google Cloud in Frankfurt. Results are stored on Cloudflare R2 restricted to the EU jurisdiction and deleted after 24 hours; the download link itself expires after one hour.

Does a video cost one credit regardless of length?

Yes, up to three minutes. Longer inputs are rejected before processing and cost nothing.

What if a job fails?

Only successful videos cost credits. If the download, the encode or the upload fails after your key was charged, the credit is returned automatically and the error response says so (credits_refunded: true).

Do credits expire? Do top-ups create a new key?

Credits never expire. Buying more with the same email adds them to the key you already have, so nothing changes in your workflow.

I lost my key.

Keys are shown once, right after checkout, and are not stored in plaintext on our side. Contact support and we will issue a new key with your remaining credits.

Can you add subtitles?

We burn in captions you supply as SRT or WebVTT — the file your transcription step already produces. That is the part that otherwise needs your own server with ffmpeg. We do not transcribe for you yet, so nothing gets misheard. One style, white with a heavy outline, placed clear of the platform interface. A captioned video costs two credits instead of one.

Does Brand-Pipe remove AI labels or watermarks?

No. Brand-Pipe formats, brands and normalises your video. Provenance metadata your generator embeds is not something we strip, and platform disclosure rules remain your responsibility.

What happens under load?

Requests beyond the service's capacity wait at the edge rather than fail; asynchronous jobs wait in a queue and are never dropped. Each key has a rate limit; a 429 means back off and retry — with an Idempotency-Key, retrying is always safe.

Can I use it without n8n?

Yes. It is a plain HTTPS API. Make, Zapier, a cron job or a shell script work just as well.
